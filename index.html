The Finding That Matters Most

Your commercial roofing page tells visitors to ring a competitor.

The commercial roofing page carries the phone number 01634 779593. That number belongs to Anticc Roofing and Building Ltd, another Chatham roofing and building firm. Commercial work is the highest-value enquiry on the site, and that page currently hands it to a rival.

It is not the only one. All ten service pages carry a leftover block reading "Images Coming Soon. For more information contact us on 07756 542 105 or email us directly at contact@brothers-carpentry.co.uk" — a third company's details. And the main homepage slider image carries a visible Anticc Roofing & Building Ltd watermark in the corner.

Taken together, the site reads as a template that was cloned and only partly rewritten. None of this is difficult to fix. All of it is costing enquiries every day it stays up.

A page that was ranking has been deleted, and the sitemap is a dead link.

/basement-waterproofing-in-kent/ returns 404. It still appears in search results with the title "Basement Waterproofing in Kent", which means it was indexed and earning visibility. Every click on that result now lands on an error page.

robots.txt tells Google the sitemap is at /sitemap_index.xml. That address also returns 404, as do /sitemap.xml and /wp-sitemap.xml. The site is pointing search engines at an empty address.

The damp content is good. The door has the wrong name on it.

But "general proofing" is not a phrase anybody types into Google. Homeowners search damp proofing, rising damp, basement waterproofing, tanking. The content is already written and already good; it is filed under a label with effectively no search demand behind it.

The Constraint Nobody Will Tell You About

The £20k commercial damp work is gated by qualification, not by marketing.

BS 8102:2022 requires a suitably qualified waterproofing specialist on the design team from the outset, accredited to at least CSSW (Certificated Surveyor in Structural Waterproofing). NHBC Standards chapter 5.4 separately requires a competent waterproofing designer and names CSSW as the example. On the mortgage-retention lane, lenders generally want a guarantee of at least ten years backed by insurance, and PCA members can issue exactly that.

Which means the specification document rules an unaccredited contractor out before price is ever discussed. No amount of advertising changes that.

The honest version: the domestic and mortgage-retention lane is winnable immediately, and it is not small. Checkatrade puts typical damp proofing around £5,000, a new damp proof course at £2,500 to £7,500, and basement waterproofing commonly at £3,000 to £8,000, rising past £8,000 to £15,000 for a full basement with drainage and a pump. CSSW and PCA are what later unlock the commercial and developer tier on top.
