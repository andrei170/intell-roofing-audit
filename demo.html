What we treat

Damp has three or four causes. Only one of them is rising damp.

Most firms sell one treatment and find a reason to use it. We are roofers as well as damp specialists, which means when the damp is actually a failed flashing or a blocked gutter, we say so rather than injecting a wall that was never the problem.

Rising Damp

Damp proof course & replastering

Tide marks, salting and perished plaster at the base of a wall. Treated at the course, then replastered to a specification that stops salts coming back through.

  • Moisture readings taken and recorded
  • Injected or physical course as the wall requires
  • Salt-retardant replastering
Basements

Tanking & cavity drain systems

Cellars and basements brought into use as dry, usable rooms. Cementitious tanking where it suits, cavity drain membrane with drainage and a sump where it does not.

  • Cavity drain membrane and channel
  • Sump and pump where required
  • Finished ready for plaster and flooring
Penetrating Damp

Damp caused by the building, not the ground

Failed flashing, cracked render, blocked gutters, a slipped tile above a chimney breast. This is where being a roofer as well as a damp contractor stops you paying twice.

  • Roof, flashing and gutter inspection
  • Render and pointing repairs
  • Fixed at source, then made good inside
Mortgage retentions

If your lender is holding money back, the clock is the problem.

When a valuer flags significant damp, lenders commonly retain part of the advance until the work is done. Releasing it usually means a specialist report and a costed remedial specification going to your solicitor, and a guarantee the lender will accept.

We deal with this every month. You will get the survey, the written specification and a clear timescale, so the sale can keep moving rather than stalling while you ring round for quotes.
